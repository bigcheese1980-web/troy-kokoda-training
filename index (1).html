Coach's essentials

✓ Technique first

Start with a manageable load, and finish most sets with 2–4 repetitions in reserve.

◉ Recovery matters

Take at least one full rest day weekly. Reduce training if joint symptoms worsen or fatigue persists.

! Medical and safety

Obtain GP clearance before structured training. Stop for chest pain, faintness or unusual breathlessness and seek medical assessment.

⌁ Trek readiness

Confirm pack weight with the trekking operator and practise hills, descents, boots, food, hydration and back-to-back days before departure.
